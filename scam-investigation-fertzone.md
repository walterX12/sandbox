# Scam Investigation: fertzone.shop

**Investigation Date:** 2026-02-10
**URL Investigated:** https://www.fertzone.shop/products/0121-4tke-o9gn

## Summary
Based on the investigation, **fertzone.shop shows multiple red flags suggesting it is likely a scam website**.

## Red Flags Identified

### 1. Website Blocked Automated Access (403 Error)
- Direct access to the website was blocked with a 403 error
- This can indicate attempts to hide content from automated scanners

### 2. No Online Presence or Reviews
- **Zero search results** specifically for "fertzone.shop"
- No reviews on Trustpilot, Reddit, or other platforms
- No mentions in scam databases
- Legitimate online stores typically have some digital footprint

### 3. Suspicious Domain Pattern
Multiple similar domain names found in scam databases with similar patterns:
- `fertus.shop` (ScamAdviser score: 65/100 - moderate trust)
- `ertzone.shop` (listed on ScamAdviser)
- `fertog.shop` (listed with concerns on ScamAdviser)
- `fertuonline.shop` (ScamAdviser score: 76/100)

This pattern suggests a **network of related scam sites** using variations of similar names.

### 4. Traffic Source: Taboola
The URL contains UTM parameters showing traffic from Taboola:
```
?utm_source=taboola&utm_medium=referral&tblci=...
```
- Taboola is a content recommendation network
- Scam sites often use paid traffic sources to drive victims to their stores
- Legitimate brands typically don't rely solely on such traffic sources

### 5. Obscure Product URL
The product URL (`0121-4tke-o9gn`) is non-descriptive and randomly generated, which is common with dropshipping scam sites.

## Recommendations

### DO NOT:
- ❌ Make any purchases from this website
- ❌ Enter credit card or personal information
- ❌ Create an account on the site

### If You Already Purchased:
1. **Contact your bank/credit card company immediately** to dispute the charge
2. Monitor your credit card statements for fraudulent charges
3. Change passwords if you created an account
4. Report to:
   - Your local consumer protection agency
   - FTC (if in the US): https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/
   - Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3): https://www.ic3.gov/

## Safe Shopping Checklist
Before buying from unknown websites:
- ✅ Check domain age (new domains are suspicious)
- ✅ Look for customer reviews on independent sites
- ✅ Verify contact information and physical address
- ✅ Check return and refund policies
- ✅ Look for HTTPS and trust badges
- ✅ Search for "[domain name] scam" online
- ✅ Be wary of prices that seem too good to be true

## Investigation Tools Used
- Web searches for reviews and legitimacy
- ScamAdviser database queries
- Domain pattern analysis
- Traffic source analysis

## Conclusion
**VERDICT: Likely Scam - High Risk**

The combination of no online presence, suspicious domain patterns, blocked automated access, and traffic from paid recommendation networks strongly suggests this is a fraudulent website designed to steal money and/or personal information.
